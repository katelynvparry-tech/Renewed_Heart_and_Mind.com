ABOUT COACH LIZ

Elizabeth M. Bethel
Coach Liz

Elizabeth M. Bethel is a life coach, author, digital creator, and founder of Renewed Heart & Mind Coaching. With over two decades of experience in Human Resources, Real Estate, and supporting people through emotional overwhelm, communication challenges, and relational patterns, she brings a grounded, compassionate approach to personal growth and healing.

Her work centers on helping people build emotionally intelligent relationships — friendships, partnerships, family dynamics, and community connections — through clarity, self-awareness, and gentle, practical tools.

As a highly intuitive coach with a deep understanding of neurodiverse and highly sensitive individuals, Coach Liz creates resources that are warm, accessible, and rooted in emotional steadiness. She lives in South Florida, where she coaches clients, writes, and spends time with her beloved dog, Koko — her in-house book critic and constant reminder that love, loyalty, and presence are the heart of every meaningful relationship.
